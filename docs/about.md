I'm driven by a curiosity about my own capabilities. I'm inspired by people who think the same way. 

I began my studies in Georgia Tech's Online Master of Science in Computer Science (OMSCS) program in early 2026. I realized I am living in an age of exciting innovation, and I wanted to get in on the fun. I'm interested in shaping a better future. Before my interests shifted to building software, I executed an MBTA pilot project involving license plate recognition cameras to clear busways at my first company out of college. Now at my second company, I am responsible for the software integration of a strategic radar.

I also cycle and weight train regularly. I most recently completed the [Marin Double Metric Century (127 miles, 10,000+ft)](https://www.strava.com/activities/19562459563). Before cycling, I was an amateur runner who completed my first marathon in [under 3 hours](https://www.strava.com/activities/10981019147). I've dabbled in sports my entire life, and have no plans to stop anytime soon.

I am an observer and a thinker, proudly blazing my own trail. My parents came to the states in the late eighties and early nineties, having left rural Vietnam. As long as I can remember, life has been a continuous game of figuring things out. I have nothing to lose, and everything to gain.
