# Why writing? Why not another medium?

Writing is an easy way to share lessons learned, can be parsed/skimmed easily, and doesn't require much tinkering. I chose the mkdocs website as a template for my blog because it takes my markdown and automatically converts it to html. The docs can be as deep or as shallow as I want them to be; there is space for notes and space for deeper thought pieces. In the future, I will explore voice dictation to remove friction and save time. I also enjoy hearing my own voice.

## Who am I, the writer?

A learner and a sharer.

## Who am I writing for?

Future me, to see the progress I have made. If you are not me, hopefully there is value for you too!
