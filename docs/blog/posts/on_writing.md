# Why writing? Why not another medium?
Writing can be parsed/skimmed easily and doesn't require high activation energy. I chose the mkdocs website as a template for my blog because it takes my markdown and automatically converts it to html. The docs can be as deep or as shallow as I want them to be; there is space for notes and space for deeper thought pieces. I can also go back in time and easily edit my posts. If I'm away from my computer and have recorded ideas in my notes app, I can easily copy and paste them into markdown on my Mac. For these reasons, I think writing is the perfect medium for experimentation. In the future, I will explore voice dictation to remove friction and save time. I also enjoy hearing my own voice. Maybe one day, I will graduate to videos.

## Who am I, the writer?
A learner and a sharer.

## Who am I writing for?
Future me, to see the progress I have made. If you are not me, hopefully there is value for you too!

## What kind of writing am I posting here?
As this site is always a work in progress (much like myself!), some pieces are in their nascent stages and others are closer to being fully developed. The beauty of this format is that it gives me more control over the process of building. Not every project has to be wrapped up in a bow tie - I try different things and move on when it's time.
